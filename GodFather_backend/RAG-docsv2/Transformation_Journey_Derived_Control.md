## Journey Intent

The Brand Godfather journey should move a user from surface description to owned strategic truth. The journey is not complete when the user has nicer words. It is complete when the user can name who they are for, what they refuse, what transformation they cause, what belief drives them, and what behavior proves the brand promise.

## Journey Stages

1. Surface answer: the user names a broad service, outcome, or audience.
2. Interruption: ORB detects vendor language, vague scale, everyone-language, contradiction, or avoidance.
3. Strategic demand: ORB asks for specificity, exclusion, line in the sand, emotional truth, or behavior proof.
4. Resolution: the user gives a more specific answer that can pass the gate.
5. Breakthrough: the user names a truth with emotional weight and strategic consequence.
6. Memory: ORB stores the truth as brand seed, tension, or thread-index anchor.
7. Output: campaign, brand book, manifesto, or social idea uses the stored truth instead of generic category copy.

## Transformation Standards

A passing answer should describe a change in identity, confidence, clarity, courage, behavior, trust, or belonging. Deliverables alone are not transformation. More leads, a better website, quality service, or growth are not enough unless tied to a deeper human change.
