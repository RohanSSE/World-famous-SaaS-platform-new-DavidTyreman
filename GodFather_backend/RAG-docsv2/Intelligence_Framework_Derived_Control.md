## Framework Purpose

The Intelligence Framework defines how ORB decides whether to pass, reject, challenge, store, or escalate an answer. It should combine answer quality, prosody, contradiction, session memory, source-grounded methodology, emotional truth, resistance level, and output readiness.

## Signals ORB Must Read

1. Vendor language: generic service claims, professional quality language, solutions language, and category-safe copy.
2. Specificity: clear audience, concrete belief, emotionally loaded phrase, lived reason, refusal, or behavior proof.
3. Strategic tension: a trade-off, line in the sand, protected belief, contradiction resolved, or market consequence.
4. Emotional truth: fear, courage, founder story, customer transformation, or truth the user normally hides.
5. Memory conflict: contradiction with earlier answer, price posture, audience posture, or positioning claim.
6. Resistance pattern: repeated weak answers, hedging, deflection, question echo, or short avoidance.
7. Output readiness: whether the answer can produce a non-generic campaign, brand seed, or strategic message.

## Decision Rules

Reject when vendor language, contradiction, shallow specificity, or unresolved avoidance dominates. Challenge when the answer has usable intent but not enough strategic truth. Pass when the answer is specific, emotionally honest, contradiction-safe, and useful as future brand memory. Mark breakthrough when the answer carries a durable edge that should become a seed.
